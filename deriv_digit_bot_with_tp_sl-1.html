2. Trading settings

Take profit and stop loss are session-level net P/L thresholds. Set either value to 0 to disable that threshold. When a threshold is reached, the bot stops opening new trades; any open contract still settles normally. Martingale example: $1 stake × 2 after a loss = $2, then $4. “Maximum increases” limits the sequence length.
